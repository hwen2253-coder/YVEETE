My Reflection

Our last group project made me reflect on both the limitations of social media research and students’ use of AI. We found it difficult to determine whether users were AHC students or University of Leeds students, as their profiles and posts often lacked clear information. This reminded me to be cautious about drawing conclusions from online identities. Our findings also suggested that many students rely heavily on AI in their everyday lives. Although AI can be useful and convenient, I worry that excessive reliance could reduce opportunities to develop creativity and independent thinking. This encouraged me to reflect on my own habits and to use AI as a tool to support my ideas while continuing to question, evaluate and think for myself.
